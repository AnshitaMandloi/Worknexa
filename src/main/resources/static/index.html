How it works

Three layers decide every request

Each layer has one job, so decisions stay fast when they are safe and careful when they are not.

1

AI recommends

Every request gets a quick risk assessment with a confidence score and the reasons behind it. If the AI service is unavailable, a built-in fallback keeps requests moving.

2

Rules constrain

Your admin sets priority-based rules on amount, documents or risk level. A mandatory rule always overrides the AI, so policy is never up for negotiation.

3

Humans decide

Anything that is not safe to automate goes through a multi-step chain of reviewers. Approve, reject or ask for more information, all with comments.

Features

Built for governance, not just speed

Multi-tenant by design

Each organization gets its own users, workflows and rules. Tenant identity comes from the signed login token, so one company can never see another’s data.

Configurable workflows

Define stages, assign roles and write business rules from the admin screen. Start from the expense template or build your own request type.

Separation of duties

Nobody can approve their own request, and only the assigned role can decide a step. Reject at any stage and the remaining steps are cancelled.

Append-only audit trail

Submissions, AI results, triggered rules, assignments and decisions are all recorded with who did what and when.

Ask for more information

Reviewers can pause a request and ask the requester for details. Once they reply, the review picks up where it stopped.

Live dashboard

See what is waiting for you, how requests are distributed by state and what share was approved without a human.
